Admissions Sequence & Rejection Dynamics

Can I apply without a visa? Can I be rejected after acceptance?

Consular Law
1
Rule 1: You MUST Apply to the Master's FIRST

You cannot apply for a European student visa beforehand. Consular portals strictly require an unconditional Letter of Admission, UK CAS code, French EEF validation, or Italian Universitaly pre-enrolment approval to open a visa case.

2
Rule 2: Can You Be Refused Even If Accepted? YES

Universities evaluate purely academic merit. Consulates evaluate state security, solvency, and immigration intent. Admission confers zero legal right to entry.

Primary Grounds for Student Visa Rejection:
  • Nationality Sanctions/Decrees: Czechia bans Russian passport holders by law; Poland enforces heavy restrictions.
  • Sanctioned SWIFT Transfers: Solvency wired from VTB, Sberbank, or Tinkoff triggers immediate compliance rejection.
  • Missing Legalizations: Bachelor diplomas or FBI/police records lacking the Hague Apostille.
  • Mathematical Solvency Flaws: Breaking the strict UK 28-day holding rule or failing the German Sperrkonto amount.
